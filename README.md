I am a molecular ecologist and use principles of population genetics and genomic data to advance conservation, with particular interests in the demographic consequences of inbreeding, local adaptation, and genetic variation in threatened populations. I am currently a Research Scientist in the Department of Biology (Funk lab) at Colorado State University and was previously a career Research Geneticist in the National Marine Fisheries Service and NOAA's Northwest Fisheries Science Center (Seattle). I held postdoctoral research positions at Uppsala University (Sweden) and the University of Montana. My papers can be found here: https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=gRys2TkAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao.
